Vegetable Soup

A hearty, comforting soup filled with fresh vegetables simmered in a savory broth. Perfect for a light yet nourishing meal.

Ingredients

  • carrots
  • potatoes
  • onions
  • green beans
  • garlic
  • salt
  • herbs
  • pepper
  • broth
  • oil

Preparation Steps

  1. Chop carrots, potatoes, onions, and green beans.
  2. Sauté onions and garlic in oil.
  3. Add chopped vegetables and broth.
  4. Season with salt, pepper, and herbs.
  5. Simmer until vegetables are tender.
Category Dinner
Serving 4
Prep Time 30 Minutes
